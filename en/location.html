Location & directions

Right on the beach promenade, opposite the lighthouse – the beach, the Alter Strom and the station are all within walking distance.

By train

Take an intercity train to Rostock Hbf, then the S-Bahn to Warnemünde. From the station, cross the station bridge and walk along the Alter Strom: about 500 m to the hotel.

By cruise ship

The Warnemünde cruise terminal is within walking distance along the Alter Strom. We are happy to store your luggage.

By car

From Berlin: A19 to the end, through the Warnow tunnel (toll) to Lütten Klein, then B 103 to Warnemünde.
From Hamburg: A1 to the Lübeck junction, A20 towards Rostock, exit Rostock-West, B 103 to Warnemünde.

Parking

Since 2018 Am Leuchtturm has been a pedestrian zone. You may drive up to the hotel to load and unload. Afterwards, park in the Kurhaus underground garage (approx. 400 m, on the promenade) at the hotel guest rate of €14 per 24 hours.

By plane

Rostock-Laage airport. For a shuttle to the hotel, please contact us: +49 381 54370.
