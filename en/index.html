Since 1850

The first hotel in town

In 1850 H. F. Ohlerich built the "Hotel Pavillon" here – just as Warnemünde was becoming a seaside resort. In 1897 the lighthouse was built right in front of it. Today an owner-run hotel continues the story.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Your room is ready from 3 pm on the day of arrival and available until 11 am on the day of departure. The reception is staffed around the clock.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Room rates are without breakfast. You can add the breakfast buffet in our breakfast lounge, overlooking the promenade, when you book.

Where can I park?

Am Leuchtturm is a pedestrian zone; you may drive up to the hotel to load and unload. Afterwards, park in the Kurhaus underground garage (approx. 400 m) at the hotel guest rate of €14 per 24 hours – tickets are available at reception.

Until when can I cancel free of charge?

Hotel bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 14 days before arrival, apartments up to four weeks before arrival.

Is there a minimum stay?

Yes, depending on the season: 2 nights at weekends in low season, 4 nights in mid season, 6 nights in high season and 5 nights over Easter, Whitsun, Ascension Day and New Year's Eve. Shorter stays can be booked at short notice, subject to availability.

Are pets allowed?

Out of consideration for all guests, we kindly ask you not to bring pets.

Can I stay before or after a cruise?

Yes. The cruise terminal in Warnemünde is within walking distance. Ask us about luggage storage and a late check-out, subject to availability.

How do I get here by train?

Take the S-Bahn from Rostock Hbf to Warnemünde. From the station it is about 500 metres on foot, across the station bridge and along the Alter Strom.
