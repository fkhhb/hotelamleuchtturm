Hanse Sail 2027 · 5 to 8 August

Hanse Sail from the hotel

During Hanse Sail, tall ships and traditional sailing vessels enter the harbour of Warnemünde – right by the lighthouse, right in front of the hotel.

The best rooms for it

  • Terrace Double Room – depending on the room, a view of the lighthouse and harbour entrance, private 12 m² roof terrace
  • Comfort Double Room – side sea view or view of the lighthouse and harbour entrance
  • Panorama Terrace Double Room – direct sea view, 40 m² roof terrace with beach chair

Good to know

  • Hanse Sail falls in the high season: minimum stay 6 nights, shorter stays at short notice, subject to availability.
  • 2027 rates are not yet published. For orientation, high season 2026: from €229 to from €339 per room and night, excl. breakfast.
  • Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival.
