All year · Warnemünde Cruise Center

Before or after your cruise

Warnemünde is one of the major cruise ports on the Baltic Sea. The cruise centre is next to Warnemünde station – within walking distance of the hotel across the station bridge (about 500 m to the station).

Your night by the sea

  • By train: intercity to Rostock Hbf, then the S-Bahn to Warnemünde
  • Rooms from 3 pm, check-out until 11 am – reception open around the clock
  • Evening walk on the beach, breakfast overlooking the promenade
  • Luggage storage and late check-out on departure day Proposal

Please note: a minimum stay applies in high and mid season; single nights can be booked at short notice, subject to availability.
