A hotel since 1850

How it all began

Today's Hotel am Leuchtturm stands on foundations from 1850 – and has witnessed more than 170 years of Warnemünde history.

  1. 1850

    H. F. Ohlerich builds the single-storey "Hotel Pavillon" as Warnemünde begins to develop into a seaside resort – the first hotel in town.

  2. ~1880

    Almost 30 years later the building is extended to three storeys.

  3. 1897

    The lighthouse – Warnemünde's landmark – is built right in front of the hotel.

  4. until 1920

    The spa garden lies in front of the hotel, a popular destination. The ground floor houses a restaurant, and the beach hall is a popular dance venue.

  5. 1950

    The last owner, Elisabeth Colas, is expropriated in "Aktion Rose". The hotel is renamed "Meer des Friedens" (Sea of Peace).

  6. after 1989

    The hotel is returned to Ms Colas, who leaves it to a parish.

  7. 1996

    Extension from 20 to 30 rooms; the first floor gets a wraparound roof terrace.

  8. 2013

    Last change of ownership – the name "Hotel am Leuchtturm" is kept.

  9. 2017

    Complete renovation and veranda extension with new reception, lobby with fireplace and a breakfast lounge overlooking the promenade.
