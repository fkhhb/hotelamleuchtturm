Panorama Double Room

Full of light, with a direct view of the beach and the Baltic Sea – at sunset one of the finest views in Warnemünde.

Spacious and comfortable in classic-modern design, furnished in boutique style with high-quality materials in light, warm tones. The elegant black-and-white marble-look bathroom has a shower or shower and bathtub and a cosmetic mirror.
